# Solar Engineering Study Material

Comprehensive collection of solar photovoltaic (PV) engineering study materials, national electrical codes, standards, guides, and technical publications.

## Contents

### 1. National Electrical Codes (NEC) & Industry Standards
- **NFPA 70 / NEC 2020 & 2023**: National Electrical Code standards
- **IEEE Standards**: 
  - IEEE 142-2007 (Grounding of Industrial and Commercial Power Systems)
  - IEEE 1547-2018 (Interconnection and Interoperability of Distributed Energy Resources)
  - IEEE 241-1990 (Electric Power Systems in Commercial Buildings - Gray Book)
- **NEMA VE 2-2018**: Metal Cable Tray Installation Guidelines
- **UL 1741**: Inverters, Converters, Controllers and Interconnection System Equipment

### 2. Mike Holt Electrical Engineering Series
- Mike Holt's Illustrated Guide to Basic Electrical Theory
- Mike Holt's Illustrated Guide to Understanding the National Electrical Code (2020) — Volumes 1, 2, and 3
- Mike Holt's Illustrated Guide to Understanding Requirements for Bonding and Grounding (2020 NEC)

### 3. Grounding & Ground Fault Protection
- Practical Guide to Electrical Grounding & System Grounding Study Reports
- Grounding and Ground Fault Protection (MDGF Protection Systems)
- Mersen PV Ground Fault Analysis
- Cadweld Welding Specifications & Grounding Bank Calculations

### 4. Cable Tray Design & Cable Management
- B-Line Cable Tray Design Considerations
- Cable Tray Engineering Calculations & Management Catalogs
- Cable Cleats Catalogs & Marked Sections

### 5. Solar Handbooks, Certifications & Guides
- **NABCEP Study Guide** (Revised Version 3)
- **Photovoltaic Technology for Hot and Arid Environments**
- **PV Installation Guide** (Latest CEC)
- **Designer Handbook v1** & Siemens Basics of Electrical Engineering

### 6. Solar Pro & Solar Power World Technical Publications
- Archive of **SolarPro Magazine** issues and technical articles
- **Solar Power World (SPW)** issues and Top Solar Contractors editions
- **Renewable Energy Handbooks** (2018–2022)
